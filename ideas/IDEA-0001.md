# IDEA-0001 — First Hypothesis

## Status: DRAFT

## The Idea

Does unusual trading volume combined with a strong price move 

predict continued short-term price movement over the next 5 days?

## Why It Might Work

When a stock moves significantly on high volume, it may signal 

institutional activity or new information entering the market.

## What We Need

- Daily OHLCV data (Open, High, Low, Close, Volume)

- At least 5 years of data

- A universe of liquid stocks

## What Would Kill This Idea

- The effect disappears after transaction costs

- It only works in bull markets (not regime-robust)

- It is just capturing market beta
